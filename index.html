The Biodiversity Dilemma in the Pilbara: Balancing Mining Boom and Subterranean Conservation

Core Dilemma & Spatial Conflict

The Pilbara region in Western Australia spans 507,896 km² (twice the land area of the UK). It is both an economic superpower generating A$140 billion in annual iron ore exports (98% of Australia's total) and a global subterranean biodiversity hotspot harboring over 4,500 endemic stygofauna (groundwater-dwelling) and troglofauna (air-breathing underground) species, over 90% of which are restricted to tiny geographic ranges (<1,000 km²).

Open-pit iron ore mining operations lower groundwater tables by tens of meters and physically excavate subterranean habitats, directly threatening non-renewable evolutionary ecosystems while encroaching on sacred First Nations cultural landscapes.

7 Landscape-Scale Governance Directives

Directives 01 - 02
Integrated Science & Cumulative EIA
Transition from isolated, project-by-project environmental impact assessments to mandatory cumulative, regional-scale hydrogeological and subterranean biodiversity modeling.
Directives 03 - 05
Refuge Networks & Indigenous Co-Governance
Establish interconnected subterranean ecological reserves, mandate aquifer re-injection protocols, and guarantee First Nations FPIC (Free, Prior, and Informed Consent) decision-making authority.
Directives 06 - 07
Financial Assurance & Global Standards
Implement ring-fenced long-term mine closure rehabilitation bonds and align regional reporting with TNFD (Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures) standards.

Industry Context & Supply Chain Vulnerability

While global pharmaceutical leaders transition aggressively toward net-zero (Pfizer 46% direct emission cut, Amgen $750M green bond, Nordic green tenders reducing CO2 by >21%), Vietnamese pharmaceutical companies face critical structural bottlenecks. Approximately 90% of active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) must be imported (primarily from China and India), exposing the domestic market to global supply shocks. Furthermore, only 17 out of 250 factories in Vietnam reach advanced GMP standards (EU-GMP/PIC/S), and zero plants currently hold WHO prequalification.

8 Strategic Policy Recommendations

Directives 01 - 03
Supply Chain Oversight & Herb Conservation
End-to-end quality control, regulatory transparency, anti-smuggling, and preservation of endemic medicinal gene pools (GACP-WHO).
Directives 04 - 06
Modernization & Digital Transformation
Pharmacovigilance safety, upgrading facilities toward WHO Level 4, and legal frameworks for AI & digital health management.
Directives 07 - 08
Global Talent & Domestic Self-Sufficiency
International value chain integration, advanced talent development, and prioritizing domestic manufacturing to reduce import dependency.

To scale their circular business model, Group 06 designed a comprehensive systems architecture incorporating rigorous requirements elicitation, UML behavioral modeling, and a 15-class domain structure.

House Price Prediction using Deep Neural Networks

Dataset Exploration & Preprocessing

The original dataset containing ~20,000 housing observations underwent rigorous data cleaning. Outliers above the 99th percentile threshold (~$1,950,000) were removed to stabilize variance, resulting in a refined dataset of 19,801 observations. Features underwent Min-Max normalization (0 to 1) and were partitioned into a 70/30 train-validation split (13,860 train / 5,941 validation).

Model Benchmarking & Architectural Comparison

Eight neural network configurations (1 to 4 layers) were benchmarked against a baseline Linear Regression model ($R^2 = 0.7112, \text{MAE } \$106,419$).

Champion Architecture
Model NN4 (3-Layer Tuned)
Dense 100 (tanh + 0.2 dropout) ➔ Dense 180 (ReLU) ➔ Dense 20 (ReLU), Adam optimizer (lr=0.001, batch 64, 500 epochs). Achieved R² = 0.8445, Correlation = 0.9193, MAE = $71,705, MAPE = 14.33% with overlapping train-validation learning curves.
Key Empirical Finding
Vanishing Gradient in NN5
Model NN5 overused tanh activation across multiple layers, causing severe vanishing gradient ($R^2 = -3.2201, \text{Correlation } 0.0954$), highlighting that architectural synergy outweighs raw parameter depth.

Airbnb Victoria Review Analytics using NLP & Topic Modeling

Aspect-Based VADER Sentiment Analysis & 10-Topic LDA on 85,155 Reviews

Analytic Methodology

Analyzed 85,155 customer reviews across Victoria. Employed sentence-level tokenization, custom stopword removal, and VADER compound sentiment scoring across 9 key lodging dimensions (bathroom, host, clean, noise, cleanliness, WiFi, amenities, value, location). Applied unsupervised Latent Dirichlet Allocation (LDA) with vocabulary restricted to 5,000–20,000 terms to identify 10 core conversation topics.

Key Empirical Findings & Business Insights

Finding 01
Geographic Satisfaction Paradox
Melbourne had the highest listings (~8,000) but ranked only 7th in guest satisfaction. Yarra Ranges achieved top satisfaction (0.87 average, 0.90 median) driven by nature and views. Monash recorded lowest (0.73).
Finding 02
Dissatisfaction Drivers
Location was the only positive aspect in negative reviews (+0.044). Primary drivers of dissatisfaction were bathroom conditions (-0.036), host responsiveness (-0.032), and cleanliness (-0.020).
Finding 03
Room Type Segmentation
Private room guests prioritize host hospitality, communication, and room care (Topics 2, 3, 6); entire home guests prioritize location accessibility, independence, and group comfort (Topics 1, 4, 10).

Dataset & Deep Learning Pipeline

Evaluated 2,864 real waste images across 6 classes (Cardboard, Glass, Metal, Paper, Plastic, Vegetation). Images were resized to 100x100x3 pixels, normalized, label encoded, and split 70/30 (2,004 train / 860 test). Dense ANNs failed (best accuracy 44.88%, Kappa 0.337) because flattening 30,000-dimensional vectors destroyed spatial feature hierarchies.

Champion Model CNN4 Results

CNN4 Topology
2-Conv Block Architecture
Conv Block 1 (32 filters 3x3, MaxPool 2x2, 0.25 dropout) ➔ Conv Block 2 (64 filters 3x3, MaxPool 2x2, 0.25 dropout) ➔ Dense 128 ReLU (0.25 dropout) ➔ Softmax 6 classes with Adam optimizer.
Class-Level Precision
Vegetation F1 = 0.94
Vegetation achieved near-perfect classification (Precision 0.94, Recall 0.93, F1 0.94). Glass and Paper reached 0.70 F1. Metal (0.62) and Cardboard (0.61) had visual overlap with plastic containers under variable lighting.
