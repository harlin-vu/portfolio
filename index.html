About Me

I believe the best use of data is not simply to optimise business performance, but to create positive change for people and communities.

As a Business Analytics student at Deakin University, I am passionate about applying analytics, AI, and business strategy to solve challenges that matter. This passion has guided the projects and experiences I choose - from sustainability consulting and ESG research, to developing AI solutions for post-discharge healthcare and exploring technology-driven approaches to environmental sustainability.

These experiences have strengthened both my analytical capabilities and my belief that innovation should be human-centred, ethical, and socially meaningful. I enjoy working with multidisciplinary teams, turning complex problems into actionable insights, and designing solutions that balance business value with long-term community impact.
